# Council MCP Procedures

The procedure is the Seyag `council` skill (`/council`, `seyag:council`); this repo adds nothing to it. Load it and follow it.

If the seyag plugin is not loaded in this session (a cloud unit or the CI review action may lack it), the pointers below do not resolve: say so, and hand the procedure step to the driver or the owner instead of reconstructing it.

- CURRENT roster + model IDs: `seyag:council § Model Selection`
- Outcome shapes: a split in `seyag:council § When the Council Splits`; disagreement with Claude in `seyag:council § When Council and Claude Disagree`; empty bodies, a silent model shrinking the panel, and the respondent-count vocabulary in `seyag:council § Model Selection`
