**#2135's matcher was rewritten THREE times by review, and the third time changed the approach.** Rounds found: lazy/dot forms missing → the optional-slash open/close form (`<\/?[^>]*>`, the canonical strip-all-tags idiom) missing → grouped atoms (`<(.*?)>`, `<([^>]*)>`, `<\/?[^>]+(>|$)`) missing.
